What the numbers mean

tap a row to expand
Adds now — the number that decides the pick

Points this player adds to your starting lineup, not his raw total. A player at a position you already have covered only counts as bench, so his "adds" collapses. This is why the app will pass on a higher-scoring player for one who fills a hole.

Cost of waiting — why not take him next round

His value minus what the best player at his position is expected to add by the time your next pick comes round. Big number: he will be gone and the drop-off behind him is steep. Dash: you can comfortably wait.

VORP — why a 400-point QB can rank below a 300-point WR

Value Over Replacement. If the QB you could get in round 10 scores 300, an elite QB scoring 400 is only worth +100. If the replacement WR scores 190, a 300-point WR is worth +110. VORP is measured against the unfilled starting slots left across the whole league and is recalculated on every pick, so it rises on its own when the room drains a position.

Lasts to 2.06 — will he still be there

Chance he survives to your next pick, from his ADP and how far away that pick is. Under 25% shows red: take him now or lose him. Over 60% green: you have a genuine choice.

Tier and cliff — where the talent drops

Tiers come from real gaps in the projection curve, not round numbers. A cliff warning means only one or two players remain before a step down, and roughly that many will go before you pick again.

Run — the room is draining a position

Four or more of the last eight picks at one position. It means that position is going faster than ADP expects, so the players you were planning to wait on will not be there.

Bye — the thing that quietly loses you a week

Byes are shown everywhere and scored. Stacking several starters into one bye week means fielding an incomplete lineup that week. A warning fires at three; the engine discounts picks that would make it worse, most heavily when you would have nobody startable at that position.

Scarcity clock — 26 · −5.9

26 startable players left at that position, and about 5.9 of them get taken before your next pick. The red section of the bar is what you are about to lose by waiting.
